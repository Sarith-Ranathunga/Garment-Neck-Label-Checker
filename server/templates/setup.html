Why the allocation matters beyond counting

The allocated sizes define the closed vocabulary the recogniser parses against. Recognition of free-form label text is unreliable; recognition against a small known set is not, and that difference is what makes the artefact work. So this table is not only a quantity record — it is the constraint that the first solution objective depends on. A size allocated here that is not actually in the shipment widens the vocabulary and makes a wrong read more likely.
